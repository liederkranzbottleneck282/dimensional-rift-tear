## 💡 Tips for Best Experience

### ☀️ Lighting Matters
Make sure your face and hands are well-lit. Good lighting helps the hand tracking work more accurately. Avoid standing with bright lights or windows behind you.

### 📏 Distance from Camera
Position yourself about arm's length from your webcam. Too close and your hands might be cut off. Too far and the tracking might not pick up your movements.

### ✋ Clear Background
A simple, uncluttered background works best. If you have lots of stuff behind you, the tracking might get confused.

### 🤸 Practice Your Moves
Try different hand movements to see what triggers the rift effect. Slow, dramatic movements often work better than quick, jerky ones.

If the app opens but shows a black screen:
1. Check that your camera is connected and working
2. Close other apps that might be using your camera
3. Restart the application

### 😤 Lag or Slow Response

If the effect seems delayed:
1. Close other browser tabs and programs
2. Move closer to your Wi-Fi router if using wireless internet
3. Reduce the video quality in your camera settings
